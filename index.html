Why one letter feels so heavy

Your job, your flat and your family's visa can all hang on one piece of paper.

We're not saying this to scare you. These are the real rules, and nobody explains them when you land.

Up to 20 months

just to get an appointment

That's how long people once waited at Frankfurt's immigration office, which says the average is now about three months. Lose your place in that queue over one missing paper, and you start again.

1 month

to challenge a decision

Official decisions usually give you one month to object. The instructions are at the bottom of the letter, in the hardest German of all. Miss the date and the decision normally stands.

1 day late

can cost you more than a day

Apply to extend your permit before it runs out and you can usually keep living and working while you wait. Apply after, and that protection is no longer automatic.

Sources: hessenschau.de on the Frankfurt Immigration Office · German administrative court rules (VwGO §§ 70, 74) · Residence Act (AufenthG § 81). General information, not legal advice.
